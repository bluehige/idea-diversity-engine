# Disclaimer

Idea Diversity Engine is an ideation and documentation aid.

- Model-estimated typicality is not a calibrated probability.
- Low typicality does not establish novelty, truth, quality, feasibility, safety, or commercial value.
- Patent-related outputs are not legal advice or a patentability opinion.
- Simulated users or survey answers are not representative population estimates unless calibrated against appropriate data.
- Safety, medical, financial, legal, and other high-impact outputs require qualified human review.
- The project does not expose or require private chain-of-thought. It asks for concise, auditable rationales only.
