# Stratified VS Prompt

1. Identify the most conventional solution patterns in at most three bullets.
2. Partition the solution space into 4–8 useful semantic strata.
3. Generate at least one candidate from each selected stratum.
4. Attach model-estimated typicality and the common audit fields.
5. Remove candidates whose mechanisms are substantively identical.

Potential strata include physical structure, sensing/data, control, interaction, workflow, safety, maintenance, business model, narrative form, visual style, or user behavior. Choose strata appropriate to the task rather than copying this list mechanically.
