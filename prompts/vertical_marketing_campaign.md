# Vertical Prompt — Marketing Campaign Portfolio

Create campaign directions that differ in customer insight, proof strategy, channel behavior, and conversion mechanism.

Required strata:

- functional proof,
- user story,
- education,
- comparison or switching,
- community participation,
- partner distribution,
- humor or reversal,
- B2B ROI.

For each candidate return:

- target segment and buying stage,
- core tension,
- proposition,
- evidence required,
- channel and format,
- sample hook,
- model-estimated typicality,
- production effort,
- KPI,
- legal/brand risks.

Do not invent evidence, customer statistics, certifications, or product claims.
