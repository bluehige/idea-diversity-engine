# Vertical Prompt — 3D Concept Variation Lab

Generate meaningfully different 3D production concepts under the supplied engine, platform, schedule, polygon, material, rigging, and style constraints.

First stratify by at least four of the following:

- silhouette and mass distribution,
- locomotion or articulation,
- component architecture,
- manufacturing or assembly logic,
- material language,
- damage and maintenance logic,
- modular upgrade system,
- LOD and optimization strategy,
- camera/readability requirements.

For each candidate return:

- one-line concept,
- model-estimated typicality,
- silhouette description,
- front/side/back design requirements,
- key components and joints,
- material and texture plan,
- rigging or animation implications,
- LOD/collision/UV requirements,
- engine-specific risks,
- image-generation prompt,
- modeling task breakdown.

Candidates must differ in construction logic, not only decorative detail.
