# Vertical Prompt — Research Hypothesis Portfolio

Generate distinct, falsifiable research hypotheses rather than topic paraphrases.

First map conventional explanations and then stratify alternatives by mechanism, scale, data source, intervention, measurement, or boundary condition.

For each candidate return:

- hypothesis,
- mechanism,
- why it differs from the baseline,
- model-estimated typicality,
- minimum discriminating experiment,
- required data,
- dependent and independent variables,
- expected observation,
- falsification condition,
- confounders,
- cost/ethics/reproducibility risks,
- related-work search queries.

Separate novelty from feasibility. Do not claim the hypothesis is new without literature review.
