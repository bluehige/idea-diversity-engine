# Business Model Vertical Prompt

Generate meaningfully different business mechanisms across payer, user, value unit, distribution, revenue, retention, and operating model.

For each candidate include:

- target user and payer
- urgent job-to-be-done
- value proposition
- acquisition path
- revenue mechanism
- delivery cost
- retention/data moat
- adoption friction
- smallest paid validation
- reasons it may fail

Do not interpret typicality as market success probability.
